Hashem Is Here: Works by Anshie Kagan

Joseph Slifka Center for Jewish Life at Yale University • New Haven, CT

February 8–March 8, 2017

Hashem Is Here: Works by Anshie Kagan brought together works examining the intersections of Orthodox Jewish life, popular culture, technology, and humor. Through drawings, paper cuts, and sculptural objects, Kagan juxtaposed religious traditions and symbols with the visual language of contemporary life.

Curated with Blanksteen Fellows Elias Bartholomew and Elizabeth Kingsley
