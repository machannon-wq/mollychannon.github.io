Elevator Music 37: David Mancuso—The Loft

Tang Teaching Museum • Saratoga Springs, NY

July 28, 2018–January 6, 2019

Elevator Music 37: David Mancuso—The Loft centered on David Mancuso Presents the Loft (1999), a two-disc compilation evoking the music of Mancuso’s influential New York dance parties. Beginning in his downtown loft in 1970, Mancuso created an intimate, noncommercial gathering place where a racially, economically, and sexually diverse community could come together around music and dancing.

The project approached the Loft not simply as a chapter in the history of disco, but as a social experiment shaped by sound, space, and collective experience. Through Mancuso’s carefully constructed listening environment and wide-ranging musical selections—from soul and R&B to Latin jazz, funk, and African music—the installation explored how music could create temporary forms of community, freedom, and belonging.
