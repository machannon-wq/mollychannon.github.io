Material Matters: How Synthetics Shaped a New Era

The Wolfsonian–FIU • Miami Beach, FL

August 28, 2026–February 21, 2027

Today, synthetic products are everywhere—but that wasn’t always the case. Beginning in the 19th and 20th centuries, laboratories were centers of incredible invention, crafting entirely new classes of materials designed to improve upon nature. Plastics, rubbers, and synthetic fibers emerged not simply to replace scarce resources but as superior alternatives, engineered for durability, affordability, and versatility.

These innovations became essential to modern industrial development, new modes of transportation, and national defense in times of war. Yet alongside their remarkable benefits came significant social and environmental consequences, effects that we still live with today.

The project combines several layers of interpretation, including a touch table, community-authored labels, and a video. The touch table invites visitors to explore and compare modern natural and synthetic materials. Community voice labels bring outside perspectives to the social and environmental questions raised by the exhibition. A video examining the preservation of modern materials offers a behind-the-scenes look at the challenges these substances pose for museum staff.
