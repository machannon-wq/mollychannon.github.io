About

Molly Channon is an interpretation specialist, curator, writer, and researcher based in Miami Beach. At The Wolfsonian–FIU, she develops interpretive strategies and exhibition content across gallery, audio, and digital platforms.

Previously, she was Curatorial Assistant at the Frances Young Tang Teaching Museum and Art Gallery at Skidmore College. She holds an MA in religion and the arts from Yale University and the Yale Institute of Sacred Music and a BA in art history and philosophy from Brandeis University.
